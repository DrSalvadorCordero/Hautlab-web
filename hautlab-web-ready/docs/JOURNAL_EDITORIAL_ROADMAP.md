# HAUTLAB Journal — Editorial roadmap

Updated: 2026-09-29

## Editorial rule

The Journal is not a generic blog. Every piece must do at least one of the following:

1. Explain a clinical decision patients commonly misunderstand.
2. Separate two problems that look similar but require different management.
3. Make HAUTLAB's clinical/aesthetic criteria legible without promotional claims.
4. Answer an informational search intent without replacing or cannibalizing the transactional procedure page.

Editorial axes: **Piel / Proporción / Expresión / Fundamentos**.

## Next priority topics

### Piel

1. **Rosácea y barrera cutánea: por qué irritar más no significa tratar mejor**
   - Intent: rosacea + sensitive skin + over-treatment.
   - Must link to the existing rosacea clinical page, not compete with local treatment intent.
   - Evidence needs: phenotype-based rosacea guidance, barrier dysfunction, ocular red flags.

2. **Caída de cabello: cuándo es efluvio y cuándo conviene buscar otra causa**
   - Intent: sudden shedding vs patterned loss.
   - Scope: recognition and consultation threshold; no universal lab panel.

3. **Dermatitis seborreica: por qué vuelve y qué significa realmente “controlarla”**
   - Intent: recurrence, scalp/face, maintenance.
   - Avoid: product-list article.

4. **Barrera cutánea: cuándo una rutina “activa” se vuelve parte del problema**
   - Intent: irritation, retinoids/acids/exfoliation, recovery.
   - Strong fit with HAUTLAB criterion: fewer interventions, better indication.

### Proporción

5. **Perfil facial: nariz, labios y mentón no se leen por separado**
   - Intent: facial profile planning.
   - Distinguish proportion from adding volume to every feature.

6. **Rinomodelación: qué puede corregir y qué no debería prometer**
   - Intent: realistic limits vs surgery.
   - Must remain informational and link to the dedicated rhinomodelation page.

7. **Mentón y mandíbula: cuándo definir no significa ensanchar**
   - Intent: projection, width, lower-face balance.
   - Avoid gender stereotypes and rigid facial ratios.

### Expresión

8. **Maseteros: estética, fuerza muscular y bruxismo no son la misma indicación**
   - Intent: distinguish aesthetic lower-face contouring from pain/TMD care.
   - Requires especially careful evidence review and referral boundaries.

9. **Tercio inferior: DAO, mentoniano y platysma como sistema de fuerzas**
   - Intent: lower-face dynamics.
   - Educational anatomy; no injection map or dosing guide for the public article.

### Fundamentos

10. **Ácido hialurónico: reversible no significa libre de riesgo**
    - Intent: informed consent and realistic safety framing.
    - Cover vascular risk, nodules, edema, migration/overcorrection and limits of hyaluronidase without alarmism.

11. **Bioestimulación: qué significa producir colágeno y qué no significa**
    - Intent: separate mechanism from marketing language.
    - Complement, not duplicate, the filler-vs-biostimulator article.

12. **Una valoración estética no debería empezar por elegir un procedimiento**
    - Intent: signature HAUTLAB methodology.
    - Structure: skin / support / proportion / movement / priorities / staged plan.

## Done criteria for every article

- Search intent is distinct from existing procedure/local pages.
- Clinical claims have current, inspectable references.
- Evidence strength is not overstated.
- No individualized prescription or dosing map.
- Article schema and FAQ match visible content.
- Internal links point to the appropriate clinical page.
- Medical review is explicit before merge.
- Preview/build passes.
