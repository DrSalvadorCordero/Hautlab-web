# HAUTLAB SEO Decision Log

## Current governing decisions

### 1. Architecture before volume

Do not publish large quantities of thin content. Strengthen high-intent hubs and service pages first.

### 2. Separate cities

Mérida and CDMX must remain distinct in architecture, copy, schema, operations and measurement.

### 3. Priority topic order

1. Dermatología clínica.
2. Medicina estética.
3. Rinomodelación.
4. Toxina botulínica.
5. Rellenos faciales / ácido hialurónico.
6. Acné.
7. Rosácea.
8. Melasma.
9. Alopecia.
10. Supporting comparison and FAQ content.

### 4. Human approval boundary

Automations may prepare technical changes and draft content. Medical claims, prices, credentials, addresses, sponsored content and regulatory-sensitive copy require explicit approval.

### 5. Pull-request workflow

All automated improvements must:

- Use a dedicated branch.
- Open a draft pull request.
- Include rationale, files changed, tests, expected impact and risks.
- Avoid direct commits to `main`.
- Avoid merge or production deployment without approval.

### 6. Measurement standard

Prioritize qualified demand and conversion, not traffic alone. Evaluate performance using Search Console, analytics, booking events, WhatsApp clicks and attributable patient value when available.
