# HAUTLAB Local SEO: Mérida and CDMX

## Non-negotiable rule

Mérida and CDMX are separate geographic entities. Do not merge their location signals, pricing, schedules, addresses, availability, procedures, testimonials or schema.

## Mérida

Mérida is the principal local entity. Pages should use truthful context for the clinic, its neighborhood, access, patient journey and relevant regional demand.

Priority themes:

- Dermatólogo en Mérida.
- Dermatología clínica en Mérida.
- Medicina estética en Mérida.
- Rinomodelación en Mérida.
- Toxina botulínica en Mérida.
- Rellenos faciales and ácido hialurónico en Mérida.
- Acné, rosácea, melasma and alopecia en Mérida.

Local references must sound natural and useful. Do not create lists of nearby cities solely for keyword coverage.

## CDMX

CDMX requires its own hub and service pages only when the underlying operational facts are current and verified.

Each CDMX page must clearly state:

- Exact or appropriate location context.
- Periodicity or availability.
- Services genuinely offered there.
- Separate contact or booking logic when applicable.
- Any city-specific limitations.

Do not copy Mérida pages and replace the city name.

## NAP consistency

Name, address and telephone information must match across:

- Website.
- Google Business Profile.
- Medical directories.
- Social profiles.
- Booking profiles.
- Structured data.

Any discrepancy should be flagged before publication.

## Google Business Profile operating standard

Maintain:

- Correct primary and secondary categories.
- Accurate services.
- Current hours.
- Original photographs.
- Regular posts.
- Complete responses to genuine reviews.
- Ethical review-request process.
- Correct appointment and service URLs.

Never buy or fabricate reviews.

## Location-page quality

A location page should answer:

- What care is available there.
- Who provides it.
- How the evaluation works.
- What services are prioritized.
- How to book.
- What local patients and travelers should know.

It should not be a doorway page containing only city keywords.
