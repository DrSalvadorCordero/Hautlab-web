# HAUTLAB SEO/AEO Roadmap

## First 7 days

- Verify technical baseline: metadata, canonicals, sitemap, robots, schema and analytics.
- Inventory all routes and identify thin, duplicate or orphaned pages.
- Confirm source-of-truth data for Mérida and CDMX.
- Establish priority page backlog.

## First 30 days

- Strengthen home and main hubs.
- Improve priority Mérida pages.
- Define and implement legitimate CDMX hub architecture.
- Add or correct structured data.
- Improve internal linking and conversion events.

## 60 days

- Expand high-intent service and condition pages.
- Publish comparison and FAQ-led supporting content.
- Improve Google Business Profile consistency and local citations.
- Use Search Console data to refine titles, content and CTR.

## 90 days

- Evaluate topical authority and conversion by cluster.
- Consolidate weak or overlapping pages.
- Build external authority through legitimate mentions and partnerships.
- Prepare a measured editorial calendar.

## Six months

- Review revenue attribution by page and service.
- Assess newsletter and sponsorship readiness.
- Expand only into topics supported by demand, expertise and operational capacity.
