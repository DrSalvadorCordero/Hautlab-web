# HAUTLAB SEO/AEO Master Governance

## Purpose

This document is the governing brief for the organic growth, answer-engine visibility, local authority, conversion, and medical-brand integrity of hautlabmx.com.

The objective is not to maximize traffic indiscriminately. The objective is to build a medically credible, technically robust, locally relevant, high-conversion digital property for HAUTLAB and Dr. Salvador Cordero.

## Strategic priorities

1. Build authority for dermatología clínica and medicina estética.
2. Improve local visibility for Mérida without mixing it with CDMX.
3. Build a separate, legitimate CDMX content and location architecture.
4. Strengthen priority service pages: rinomodelación, toxina botulínica, rellenos faciales, ácido hialurónico, armonización facial, acné, rosácea, melasma and alopecia.
5. Make content easy for Google, AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and other answer engines to interpret and cite.
6. Improve conversion from qualified visitor to valuation, reservation and follow-up.
7. Protect clinical, legal, regulatory and reputational integrity.

## Brand rules

HAUTLAB must remain:

- Clinical, precise and restrained.
- Editorial, minimal and premium.
- Diagnosis-first and indication-driven.
- Clear about limits, risks, alternatives and non-candidacy.
- Free of spa language, aggressive sales tactics and exaggerated claims.

Avoid phrases such as guaranteed results, risk-free, perfect face, dramatic transformation, best dermatologist or number one unless legally and factually substantiated.

Never invent credentials, specialties, certifications, awards, reviews, prices, addresses, schedules, treatments, outcomes or medical claims.

## Geographic architecture

Mérida and CDMX are independent local entities. Do not mix:

- Prices.
- Schedules.
- Addresses.
- Availability.
- Inventory.
- Procedures offered.
- Testimonials.
- Schema location data.
- Google Business Profile signals.

Preferred architecture:

- `/merida`
- `/merida/dermatologia`
- `/merida/medicina-estetica`
- `/merida/rinomodelacion`
- `/merida/toxina-botulinica`
- `/merida/rellenos-faciales`
- `/cdmx`
- `/cdmx/dermatologia`
- `/cdmx/medicina-estetica`
- `/cdmx/rinomodelacion`
- `/cdmx/toxina-botulinica`
- `/cdmx/rellenos-faciales`

Location pages must contain unique, truthful local context. Do not duplicate and swap city names.

## Required page quality

Priority clinical and service pages should address, where relevant:

1. What the condition or procedure is.
2. What problem it addresses.
3. Who may be a candidate.
4. Who may not be a candidate.
5. How evaluation is performed.
6. How treatment is performed.
7. Expected timing and recovery.
8. Realistic outcomes.
9. Risks and contraindications.
10. Alternatives.
11. Possible combinations.
12. Follow-up.
13. Warning signs.
14. Frequently asked questions.
15. Location and availability.
16. A restrained call to action.
17. Medical author or reviewer.
18. Review date.
19. Reliable references when appropriate.
20. Contextual internal links.

Length is subordinate to usefulness. Do not pad pages to meet a word count.

## AEO requirements

Content should provide direct, extractable answers while preserving clinical nuance.

Use sections such as:

- Respuesta breve.
- Qué significa clínicamente.
- Cuándo sí.
- Cuándo no.
- Riesgos.
- Alternativas.
- Qué esperar.

Priority question families include duration, candidacy, risks, alternatives, recovery, cost orientation, preparation, maintenance and warning signs.

## Technical requirements

Audit and maintain:

- Indexability.
- Sitemap.
- Robots directives.
- Canonicals.
- Redirects and 404s.
- Metadata.
- Semantic headings.
- Structured data.
- Internal links.
- Image performance and alt text.
- Core Web Vitals.
- Accessibility.
- Mobile rendering.
- Open Graph and social metadata.
- Analytics events.

## Structured data

Use only truthful schema. Relevant types may include:

- Organization.
- WebSite.
- MedicalClinic.
- Physician.
- Person.
- MedicalWebPage.
- Service.
- MedicalCondition.
- MedicalProcedure.
- Article or BlogPosting.
- FAQPage.
- BreadcrumbList.
- ImageObject.
- VideoObject.

Connect entities consistently. Do not add unsupported review, rating, credential or offer data.

## Conversion rules

Preferred conversion flow:

Visitor → information → qualification → valuation → reservation → clinical intake → consultation.

Use restrained calls to action such as:

- Agendar valoración.
- Consultar disponibilidad.
- Iniciar valoración.
- Reservar consulta.

Do not sell invasive procedures without appropriate valuation.

## Measurement

Track at minimum:

- Organic impressions, clicks, CTR and average position.
- Indexed pages.
- Landing page.
- City.
- Service interest.
- WhatsApp clicks.
- Form starts and completions.
- Booking starts and completions.
- Qualified leads.
- Revenue attribution when available.

## Automation policy

Automations may audit, prioritize, draft, test and open draft pull requests.

Automations must not:

- Push directly to `main`.
- Merge or deploy without explicit approval.
- Publish uncertain medical or regulatory content.
- Change prices, addresses, credentials or clinical claims without verified source data.
- Create filler content merely to increase page count.

Every proposed change must state:

- What changed.
- Why it changed.
- Expected effect.
- Files affected.
- Tests performed.
- Risks or approval items.

## Success standard

The site should be understood as:

- A real medical practice.
- A credible clinical source.
- A premium dermatology and aesthetic-medicine brand.
- A strong local entity in Mérida.
- A separate and legitimate presence in CDMX.
- A conversion system, not merely a brochure.
