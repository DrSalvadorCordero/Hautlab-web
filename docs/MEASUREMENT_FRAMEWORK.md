# HAUTLAB Measurement Framework

## Core objective

Measure qualified demand and attributable patient value, not traffic alone.

## Required organic metrics

- Impressions.
- Clicks.
- CTR.
- Average position.
- Indexed pages.
- Query and landing-page performance.
- Brand vs non-brand demand.
- Mérida vs CDMX visibility.

## Conversion events

Recommended events include:

- `view_service`
- `select_city`
- `click_whatsapp`
- `start_booking`
- `complete_booking`
- `submit_form`
- `view_price`
- `faq_opened`
- `scroll_75`

## Business outcomes

When operational data is available, connect:

- Lead source.
- Requested service.
- City.
- Qualification status.
- Booking.
- Attendance.
- Revenue.
- Patient lifetime value.

## Reporting cadence

### Weekly

- Technical defects.
- Indexation changes.
- Highest-impact page opportunity.
- New local or competitor signal.

### Monthly

- Query and page movement.
- CTR opportunities.
- Conversion performance.
- Content updates.
- Next-month execution priorities.

### Quarterly

- Architecture review.
- Topic authority.
- Local entity strength.
- Revenue attribution.
- Sponsorship readiness.
