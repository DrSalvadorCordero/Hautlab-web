# HAUTLAB Sponsorship Policy

## Objective

Future monetization must preserve clinical independence and the premium character of HAUTLAB.

## Acceptable formats

Potential formats include:

- Clearly disclosed sponsored educational articles.
- Newsletter sponsorship.
- Educational guides.
- Events or reports.
- Curated product education with transparent evaluation criteria.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- Sponsored content must be labeled clearly.
- Sponsors may not dictate clinical conclusions.
- Payment may not guarantee a favorable recommendation.
- Patient data may not be shared with advertisers.
- Prescription products, injectables and restricted health products require heightened legal and regulatory review.
- Clinical service pages must not become advertising inventory.

## Approval requirement

All sponsorship, affiliate, advertising and branded-content arrangements require explicit approval and a documented conflict-of-interest review before publication.
