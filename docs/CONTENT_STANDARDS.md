# HAUTLAB Content Standards

## Editorial position

Content must sound clinical, precise, calm and premium. It should reduce uncertainty without becoming promotional, generic or spa-like.

## Medical integrity

Every clinical page must distinguish education from individual medical advice. Use cautious, accurate language and explain uncertainty where it matters.

Do not:

- Promise outcomes.
- Minimize risk.
- Present every visitor as a candidate.
- Recommend prescription treatment without appropriate context.
- Invent evidence, references, statistics or credentials.
- Use superlatives that cannot be demonstrated.

## Required content pattern

For major services and conditions, cover as applicable:

- Direct summary.
- Clinical context.
- Indications.
- Non-candidacy.
- Evaluation process.
- Procedure or treatment process.
- Expected evolution.
- Risks and contraindications.
- Alternatives.
- Follow-up.
- Warning signs.
- FAQ.
- Location-specific availability.
- Author or reviewer and review date.

## SEO and AEO writing

- One clear primary intent per page.
- One descriptive H1.
- Logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Direct answers near the relevant heading.
- Natural semantic variants; no keyword stuffing.
- Meaningful internal links.
- Unique city copy; never city-name replacement duplication.
- Meta titles and descriptions written for relevance and CTR, not hype.

## Calls to action

Preferred:

- Agendar valoración.
- Consultar disponibilidad.
- Reservar consulta.
- Solicitar orientación inicial.

Avoid scarcity, urgency and price-pressure language.

## Images

Images must be clinically and editorially relevant. Use accurate alt text that describes the image and context, not a list of keywords. Never imply a result or treatment shown if the image does not substantiate it.

## References

Use primary or authoritative medical sources when claims require support. Record review dates. Avoid decorative bibliographies that do not support the actual page.

## Sponsored content

Sponsored material must be clearly disclosed. A sponsor may support distribution or production but may not purchase a favorable clinical conclusion. Editorial and medical control remains with HAUTLAB.
